01 / Why BlackFlag

Own the library

Streaming is easy. Owning a music library is better.

BlackFlag is for FLACs, MP3s, old rips, carefully tagged albums, duplicate folders, missing artwork, and the rest of the mess that comes with keeping music for years.

It is not trying to replace your collection. It is trying to make the thing easier to live with.

03 / Rules

No library roulette

BlackFlag does not freestyle on your collection.

The risky stuff stays boring on purpose. Show the plan. Check it. Back it up. Make the change. Keep a way back.

01

No permanent duplicate delete

Cleanup moves eligible files to quarantine so they can be restored.

02

No automatic provider traffic

Online metadata and artwork lookup only runs when you ask for it.

03

No surprise metadata writes

Supported writes start with a preview, a check, and a backup.

04

No broad file reorganization

BlackFlag does not rename, sort, or rearrange your music folders on its own.
